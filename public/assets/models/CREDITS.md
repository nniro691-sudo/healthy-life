# Temple visual assets

## Principal Buddha
- `../shakyamuni-gold-hd.webp`: generated for this project with the built-in imagegen tool. A transparent image in a physical 3D shrine, not a scanned museum object or a photograph of an actual temple statue.
- Complete face, hands, robe and lotus throne. Original 1024 × 1536 resolution and transparent silhouette preserved; WebP quality 94, alpha quality 100 for web delivery.
- Generation prompt: `../shakyamuni-gold-hd.prompt.txt`.

## Bodhisattva, one of a pair, 18th century
- `bodhisattva-compact.glb`, shared by both attendants.
- Author: **Minneapolis Institute of Art (artsmia)**.
- Original: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/bodhisattva-one-of-a-pair-18th-c-ce-f7c224270f5144a99c40d6955218bac5
- Download mirror: https://www.artheonmuseum.org/artwork/bodhisattva-one-of-a-pair-18th-c-ce-f7c224
- License: **CC BY-SA 4.0**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
- Adaptation: original detailed geometry and proportions retained, painted color textures removed, physical gold material substituted. Normal and ambient-occlusion maps retained. Meshopt compression uses 16-bit positions and 12-bit normals; textures use WebP quality 95. Original 12.21 MB reduced to 2.34 MB.
